OUR . . .faith


How did I miss this???

OUR FAITH.

Interesting how many times I can pour over the same scripture and feel like I've never read it before.

It's the pronoun! I never really noticed the pronoun. Geeze. It's the pronoun.

- "YOUR FAITH has healed you."

- "According to YOUR FAITH let it be done to you."

- "And he did not do many miracles their because of THEIR lack of faith."

- "Why did YOU doubt?"

Fascinating. The text doesn't say "I healed you" . . it says "YOUR FAITH has healed you."





FAITH

DEPENDENT

UNIVERSE?

God's power.

Our faith.

What are the limits of physics?

What are the limits of faith?

What are the limits of possibility?

2 questions I ask myself . . . when someone or the world tells me something is impossible (like building a quantum AI machine to protect humanity and nature) . . .

1. Is it prohibited by the laws of physics?

2. Has this person identified a valid exception to faith? (i.e. Is the assertion that this person or the world is making that all things are not possible through faith because this one's impossible . . . a true exception to faith)

It almost always falls out under question #1 before I even get to question #2.
