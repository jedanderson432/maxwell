How does EnviroAI recognize environmental anomalies and objects?

. . . just like AI is being trained to recognize cancer and other anomalies or objects. We use machine learning and a deep learning convolutional neural network to train AI to recognize pollution and other environmental objects (smoke, sheens, nonexistent or improper control equipment, etc.)
