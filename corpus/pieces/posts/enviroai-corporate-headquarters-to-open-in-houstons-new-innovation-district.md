Exciting times in Houston. Houston is developing a new "Innovation District". [Greentown Labs](https://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=001nMbzsVX0gRKs1-Aw5astnyxH-dHxxwdSPvC7h4W5dDg8y3Jd_SpEmNZdUt-68vePsLR-9_VzIT0iKc0wxZoCd_H9MFpmpX3pRssD9kEfqk9Y0Q4srRyp4r7U7GYEMnhpcPS5Wl3IFR-AlgdBaNHUBj1cLKSxvGm6&c=&ch=) and [The Ion](https://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=001nMbzsVX0gRKs1-Aw5astnyxH-dHxxwdSPvC7h4W5dDg8y3Jd_SpEmNZdUt-68vePp3NVcKgZZ3LuaujNIuf5hwyuKXWvS0zPc6McUuZweDB2qZJXFVSsoaiFVcZdGyFOLHEON_58mmgd8tbg5-SW5g==&c=&ch=) are already open.

---"In Houston we have the Medical District, the Museum District, Downtown, and Midtown. Now we will have the Innovation District that will sit at the center of these districts. Exciting times in Houston." [(video)](https://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=001nMbzsVX0gRKs1-Aw5astnyxH-dHxxwdSPvC7h4W5dDg8y3Jd_SpEmNZdUt-68vePQ_tfMp2JWYveRsl9XMVL1IfapKhFUAw6ic6FmzPdACRip4eBXgSuFJyjeVdMTqJ9hnWYk-0H0F2Uybym0Y3bZQ==&c=&ch=)

EnviroAI was accepted to Greentown Labs last year. EnviroAI will now be locating its corporate headquarters in the Innovation District.

Initially EnviroAI will be looking to locate 100 employees in the Innovation District, with 150 employees over-seas.

"We run a a 24/7 coding operation. Our over-seas team codes at night, we turn around the code during the day. The key to technology is speed.

It's a faster world.

It's a smaller world.

It's a more integrated world.

EnviroAI stands at the forefront of connecting the environmental and digital worlds with the human world."
