1. Would you storm a machine-gun nest if you needed to? Yes.
2. If you saw someone drowning, but they were a 1/2 mile away, would you still try? Yes.
3. Would you try to build a machine to protect nature and humanity if you knew you could do it, even if you didn't yet have the means to do it? Yes.

Love will make you do some pretty stupid stuff. Hope all of you are also doing some stupid stuff for love. Short dance. It's love for God, people, and creation that endures. Love big today.
