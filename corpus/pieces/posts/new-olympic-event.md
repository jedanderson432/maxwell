New Olympic Event?

"FAITHING"


‌


I was thinking this morning that if there was a new Olympic event called "faithing" . . . the German pictured here might just be my first choice as a teammate.

How to get good at "faithing"?

1. Put yourself in dangerous and difficult positions . . . the more impossible they look, feel or are . . . the better
2. Trust only in God to pull you through, and not yourself or others (frankly, you won't have much of a choice here because that's what you will generally be left with)
3. Make faith the award. The German in the picture above was executed along with his family after this picture was taken. The award is faith itself.
