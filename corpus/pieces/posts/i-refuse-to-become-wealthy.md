. . . I refuse to become wealthy.



‌

That is where I intend to "one-up" Bezos, Musk, and other technological entrepreneurs. I will succeed without becoming wealthy. I don't think anyone's tried this before. I think it sounds like a fun challenge.

---"I might be the first entrepreneur in history with an initial plan to avoid making too much money for myself. It sounds kind of funny. But I think it will be a hoot. It's just fun to try to do something in a way that's never been done before."
