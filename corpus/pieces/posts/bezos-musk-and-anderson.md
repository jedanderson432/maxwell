Who is the least crazy?

‌

Bezos, Musk . . .

. . . my vision and implementation is the least crazy

Most companies when I first tell them about our project to remove pollution from their facilities in the Houston Ship Channel area look at me like I'm completely nuts.

![](/images/posts/bezos-musk-and-anderson/img-01.png)

I'm not trying to go to Mars.

I'm not trying to put heavy-industry into space.

I'm just piping pollution to a central facility and treating it. Not only is it not impossible . . . it's not even that difficult.
