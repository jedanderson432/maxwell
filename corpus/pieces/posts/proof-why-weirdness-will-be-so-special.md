Quantum AI Environmental Protection Machine

"Normal" Classical Physics is Just as Weird as Quantum Physics . . . let's be honest with ourselves about this fact.

99.9% of people are thinking of a quantum computer as just a more powerful computer. It's therefore hard for them to see anything special about harnessing quantum computing, and why EnviroAI could use it to help build an environmental protection machine. THAT'S NOT WHAT A QUANTUM COMPUTER IS. (Again I would point you to the links below). YOU MUST GRASP ITS WEIRDNESS.

And if you are like me and have trouble grasping quantum weirdness, here is one thought that helps me. Remind yourself how completely weird "normal" physics is around you (classical newtonian physics). I love this description from the "Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy":

---“The fact that we live at the bottom of a deep gravity well, on the surface of a gas covered planet going around a nuclear fireball 90 million miles away and think this to be normal is obviously some indication of how skewed our perspective tends to be.”-- Douglas Adams
