---"Shine your light into the world. Putting a bushel on the light isn’t modesty, it's selfishness. It's seeing the light as yourself. It's possessiveness of something that's not yours. It's putting a filter on the light. It’s in truth an unwillingness to abandon self and become translucent to the light. Shine. Shine like the stars. Shine. Shine. Shine."

- Jed Anderson, Environmental Entrepreneur
