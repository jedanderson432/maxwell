For eight years we've been building an environmental brain. 12 million documents. Three AI models. 14 states. The brain understands environmental law, permits, compliance, science. It reads and reasons about the regulatory world better than any system that exists.

But it couldn't see. 

It couldn't look at a facility and tell you what the atmosphere is doing with its emissions right now. It couldn't look at a watershed and tell you that your junior water right faces curtailment in 90 days.

That changes now.

We are connecting the environmental brain directly to the environment itself.

Real-time atmospheric modeling. Real-time watershed intelligence. Physics engines that reconstruct what's happening between a facility and the air and water around it’continuously.

Not just the facility. The environment around it.

The facility knows what it's emitting and consuming. The environment knows what it's receiving and supplying. They've never been able to see each other.
We're building the connection.

Air and water. Simultaneously. Starting now.

Why have we built systems that understand what we're putting INTO the environment’but not what the environment is doing WITH it?

The brain had knowledge. Now it gets eyes.

Onward. Upward.
