EnviroAI is expanding from a subscription model to also a [report and task model](http://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=001-EbEQpzsccEPEkazYNCl1oaac4IIAySA4aT1L8JtYKW5uuhPhEf9I45ei7OLYNKBU3G6eH8EHCCjpnYQppN0_QwZ_GS6GPBPGVZfW57joj3y5SbZfmc8p-jqA3AEH16FuozAgG4p-mNFbjRzQrLwnyX3brGtIzHkLhz3_4pufuq29q8Xo9tnlpYOMfWmyhNsq3DmuWjv7v_xd2YMvTmeZ7Yz8MPG6f3PhSRk7jd_E5U=&c=&ch=). EnviroAI will function in the near future not only as an environmental search engine, but also a voice-activated environmental assistant. Progress will be incremental with the goal of automating as many environmental protection tasks as possible. Better. Faster. Simpler. Less cost and expense to companies.
