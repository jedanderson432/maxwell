. . . and my take-aways in building a "machine" to protect earth

![](/images/posts/3-greatest-motivational-speeches/img-01.gif)

3 Greatest

﻿Motivational Speeches

![](/images/posts/3-greatest-motivational-speeches/img-02.gif)

My list of the 3 greatest motivational speeches of all time . . . along with my take-aways as a person with the imagination and goal to build a "machine" to protect the environment of life on earth.

#1 This choice will surprise you. I don't find him inspirational. But he might just be though the most motivated human being to ever have lived.

[![](/images/posts/3-greatest-motivational-speeches/img-03.png)](https://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=001NptoOArNb1oIyyC-OmEi74gtJBjMezhN35_9pyO8I7MiHpHK6YpZKqbF2NdcGado1cVF03-FpzzXfvUlpAFaTdNks9022gOun-fXeyZt14dE39orV7hll7bD33_PU8GWkbFrVLSD9o1qVOrSrFJilVz4nYEIU5s4hm7L3Hwy9YWCqr2bJLd8Qw==&c=&ch=)

Takeaways:  No Plan B. Ok to fail, but you cannot have a Plan B. NO PLAN B.

#2 This choice will not surprise you.

[![](/images/posts/3-greatest-motivational-speeches/img-04.png)](https://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=001NptoOArNb1oIyyC-OmEi74gtJBjMezhN35_9pyO8I7MiHpHK6YpZKqbF2NdcGadovExGimigi26rlQBliaqwWii5tVNNux2fJQLPVvhgDxlLPM51UgPhoZ59tukBjBvuYs3xTD6SzqndzxG7nItHzw==&c=&ch=)

Takeaways: You will die. Russia will launch a nuclear attack, you will get covid or cancer, or you will just die of old age . . . whatever it is . . . you will die soon. Remember this everyday. Smile at it. Let it motivate what you do with your precious little time on earth.

#3 This choice should not surprise you.

[![](/images/posts/3-greatest-motivational-speeches/img-05.png)](https://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=001NptoOArNb1oIyyC-OmEi74gtJBjMezhN35_9pyO8I7MiHpHK6YpZKqbF2NdcGadoKRhTdaO4qrz3lLn3st5TXXemV1jVQFEXwGOGy1lxsSoZH3FyGhxjyPEVS0z55OxkUJc5UeoIyoJOGav9n5z-uA==&c=&ch=)

Takeaways:  J.K. Rowling focuses only two. Just two. Only two factors that are important. Just two. 1. Failure. 2. Imagination.

![](/images/posts/3-greatest-motivational-speeches/img-06.gif)

![](/images/posts/3-greatest-motivational-speeches/img-07.gif)

Summary of 3 Speeches:

1. No plan B.
2. Going to die.
3. Fail. Imagine.

--JK Rowling
