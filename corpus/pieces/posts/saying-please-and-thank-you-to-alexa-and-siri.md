We created EnviroAI to teach AI that humans value nature . . . that humans value humanity. That nature and humans are worth the machine helping to protect. Mr. Gawdat's idea to train AI to do good things in this world, and to behave well in front of AI and show AI good things to train on, is very good AI parenting advice.
