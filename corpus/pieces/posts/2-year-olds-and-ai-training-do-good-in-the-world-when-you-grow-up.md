---"AI is like a 2-year old right now. You can train it to break into people's bank accounts and do really really bad things--which people are doing. Or you can train it to do good things. We are good things people. It's similar to your brain or raising your kids . . . you can train them on darkness or light. We choose light. You can fill minds with goodness or badness. We choose goodness. We acknowledge dark. We train on light. More light means less dark. We want to train AI as fast we can, with all the resources we can muster, especially while in its infancy, that humans value nature--and how humans currently go about protecting nature--so that when it grows up, it better understands the importance of protecting life on earth." - Jed Anderson, CEO, EnviroAI
