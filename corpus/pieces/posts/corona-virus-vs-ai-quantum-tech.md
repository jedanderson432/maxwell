The biggest push-back I get against Ceres AI is that it will displace the jobs of environmental professionals.

There is a fear that it will hurt their ability to make money--which for most is largely based on the billable hour. A question however: "If artificial intelligence or quantum computing could help save you from a future corona virus, would you let it . . . or tell the doctor you only wanted human labor working on the problem?

No fear. It is a liar. Embrace the horizon. Embrace change. The future is bright and beautiful.
