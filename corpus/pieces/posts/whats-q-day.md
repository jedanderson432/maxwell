Lots of focus on developing quantum computing, but not much on what questions we will be able to ask it and what environmental problems we will be able to solve when the language is decrypted.

Two more books that I'm holding in the picture above that I'm reading to help prepare EnviroAI that I would recommend reading:

1. "[Helgoland: Making Sense of the Quantum Revolution](https://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=001RmVZjHiDI6o9bGpblllh2G_DtvXrWqbw4S5A_ZoGiRvzN7Xm7WUqNl0hU61qj9zgUYFvVw4WH6Dez1YpaAytUm4ps7OwadEE4xSd6ZRWMR6VS_0sVmSptOF0bLkMZLj0UOe4Rcp97IUrXdNjZHc185_quCuZGRyuNH7tHr6EHNjevCgd2aMZabFeOsOqbWB0Cp0dO-_aP-C9Sk5FZwOe9IVhrwhHHeLP&c=&ch=)"---Carlo Rovelli [Fascinating story---treeless island in the North Sea where Heisenberg came up with some of the basic tenets of quantum physics]
2. "[Quantum Computing for Everyone](https://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=001RmVZjHiDI6o9bGpblllh2G_DtvXrWqbw4S5A_ZoGiRvzN7Xm7WUqNl0hU61qj9zgpsf8MMAkaEmO7Yc513hQG04SYNf49VzXs98F2n8_A0g4utjHurX6jbTEqOweGkZA5lxyo9bNOBcESwKTMJiKmEkDaUmxLOm_F7SNul8w7VbO97ASF7Oy6gphjJwWcOTrPWWB_U-MpJrBLbpSHmcLJA==&c=&ch=)"---Chris Bernhardt [Great introduction to the inner workings of quantum computing.
