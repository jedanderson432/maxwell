Yesterday i did a job for a client that would have taken me 50 hours without EnviroAI search. Instead I was able to complete the job in less than 6 hours.

The client asked me to research companies handling a certain feedstock, what companies were doing with the feedstock, how they were transporting the feedstock, and the amounts of feedstock being utilized.

The net savings to the client using EnviroAI for this job was $15,750. And I was able find information that otherwise I never would have found. Also, the job was completed in just over a 1/2 of a day rather than a week.
