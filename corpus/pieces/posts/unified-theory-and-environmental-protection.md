"A Theory of Everything . . . including Consciousness"

A unified field theory of general relativity and quantum mechanics is important to more fully understand the environment, but protecting the environment requires us to also understand something that may or may not be entirely physical---consciousness. Understanding a tree is important. Understanding a human understanding a tree is important.

Significant strides have occurred recently in our understanding of consciousness [(example)](https://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=001momwLc-kSbOYssIy4nUukU1d7HNphWAa6X5W4FKxxvQda8f2EfB33Spb56v5tP8H-A7ukcBvnd30GukX9ooHMWUjrSSZqR0_XSWGQVjKyotZs6TR-f2U83NTdhJeR0UA4IPUul_i3aMLzhM0CbY1RSGM6M69O-QRcyAjffUDU3NTcLiPD2MK05aHvbmgdtpgDtjSpJe05zs=&c=&ch=)---even research into whether consciousness is uniquely a human feature: [MIT article](https://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=001momwLc-kSbOYssIy4nUukU1d7HNphWAa6X5W4FKxxvQda8f2EfB33Spb56v5tP8Hagpi1yZ9rgL1BCcHSu7vf4t58nMVXSJnrK_2pGlbeRrVQ0U2T-P1XyLcQwyu41HtQJZfoCkt7qf-6_726YoG0d5gVatT7VdpcMRB9neuNhUzsrr_KTtE0EKKLatEYPiiIAfh79pGBM8ZLvqFy24mKAhKaZcdbgf3be5z_MH0SBE=&c=&ch=)).
