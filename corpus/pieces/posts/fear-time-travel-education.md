Fear is the stupidist thing in the universe(s).

I got a devotional about fear yesterday (see below).

Got me thinking about antidotes.

Faith, yes.

Courage, yes.

And here's a less obvious antidote to fear . . . EDUCATION.

---"By deepening our understanding of the true nature of physical reality, we profoundly reconfigure our sense of ourselves and our experience of the universe."- [Brian Greene](https://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=001Js7i-nacKIxVEi2qGbJVQ9QNY856e4sUjJutnYwYpoI7g08RctneKSM4Wet6FVbZIkMjWpfQKBJify68xbInOCHb-_YckQFCwHF_NpFeK9A2t97d_0RQ9PJE9SxGLRI4ATUVqCDdgpt61oGIQdAOU9ujHc-drSo-IsfL_20l6fuu__ghpaPCuXXyTyW-sXg4NrxORVrNtHM=&c=&ch=)

The more we learn about life in the universe(s), we see it less as scary and more just really, really, really weird. And I don't mean a little bit weird, I mean WEIRD.

No fear . . . it's just weird.

And perhaps the weirdest thing is this: Why are so many things possible? That's what baffles me. Why do the laws of physics allow so many things to be possible? It's not impossibility. It's possibility. That's what's baffling. WHY ARE SO MANY THINGS POSSIBLE? They seemingly shouldn't be. But they are. Baffling.

I bumped into this quote the other day . . .

---"I myself believe that there will one day be time travel because when we find that something isn't forbidden by the over-arching laws of physics we usually eventually find a technological way of doing it." - David Deutsch, Oxford University  [Father of the Quantum Compter]

The end of this quote bears repeating 3 more times . . .

. . .because when we find that something isn't forbidden by the over-arching laws of physics we usually eventually find a technological way of doing it."

2 questions I ask myself . . . when someone or the world tells me something is impossible (like building a quantum AI machine to protect humanity and nature) . . .

1. Is it prohibited by the laws of physics?

2. Has this person identified a valid exception to faith? (i.e. Is the assertion that this person or the world is making . . . that all things are not possible through faith because this one's impossible . . . a true exception to faith)

It almost always falls out under question #1 before I even get to question #2.

10 "Reality Calisthenics . . . An Antidote for Fear"

, # 1 - While sitting in your chair . . . think about the fact that you are in "reality" spinning at 1,000 mph around earth . . . twirling around the sun at 67,000 mph . . . circumnavigating the galaxy at 490,000 mph . . . and pirouetting around the universe at 1.3 million mph. In "reality" you are moving very, very, very, very, very, very fast! The only reason you don't feel this speed is that everything else around you is moving at the same speed

---"At this very moment you are moving at over 1.3 million miles per hour around the universe."

- Jed Anderson

#2 - Without doing anything, realize you actually are falling at 9.8 meters per second/per second. Earth is just getting in the way. ([General Theory of Relativity](https://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=001Js7i-nacKIxVEi2qGbJVQ9QNY856e4sUjJutnYwYpoI7g08RctneKbwsiBzgiw9W5iYk8pbJfA81GLs00HRGQVFrmrFszfwtdjRBqdv12IS_fwgQA9i_k4TVBnJvlI9n-zqlDMZKn2ERYfrKQoEiW5X5wPluK4dlXvG9pSh6ZstDxZO32QovaLaBnAoCN8x3lIjHLrBR59UQW9hiMXNuqUhrmMEiOwvzOJesbXC7Pk4Fw6xrhPrma8QhkMtwnHt3lh8e7LYk78c=&c=&ch=))

#3 - Go on a walk. Time is now moving slower for you. ([Special Theory of Relativity](https://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=001Js7i-nacKIxVEi2qGbJVQ9QNY856e4sUjJutnYwYpoI7g08RctneKbwsiBzgiw9WuY1lm9DzqRSIBlJ5Z26efs8OInRvI8dBETqmKTV4g0TFikx05auX9IAtUEkOELbbiBsEbmNWEpdWoe9QKM-1nFrbFOTRslfqHjpqNJ64-DANDzuYFCts23r3azqSI2_2&c=&ch=))

#4 - Stand on your tip-toes. Time is now going faster for your head. ([General Theory of Relativity](https://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=001Js7i-nacKIxVEi2qGbJVQ9QNY856e4sUjJutnYwYpoI7g08RctneKbwsiBzgiw9We8T7Bo1oLg-zTn0FGp5J-fyk1gVUGFklFmdjGVwXnaJxAilKo3uel_ZpQ6IrDhBh_zq0eCOqWtAzH_bxtFAxKS7IuQx6ppfCZ0wH6cs0VTsH-WrQb0HSeIk3jxeJbnjY72mMT0V9DMG6S3JUDgmj41zOISXVBgZ9o9hGq7O_DXNYyvLybXg45GLTzA6NMWxW&c=&ch=))

#5 - Look at your desk and realize its mostly space and not matter. ([electromagnetic field)](https://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=001Js7i-nacKIxVEi2qGbJVQ9QNY856e4sUjJutnYwYpoI7g08RctneKbwsiBzgiw9WHP5v8GMz3FL_t9K4tuW6e3dIlPS2E-fRQGzwPgBOGo6mnf8tEwRB6SFq3Lb6aCBYh-pe9sQZ0qqUKJwvwRuc0k9NOlG_7n_8RcvNW0llpY3nDQsZaIfo7Ip1EOvWpDM5nL5bliUou6hzgO58aqyvEsdhvzkGvM5EX-2Rhy8BgQsreEmUg3dHoy5Ptvm5pBib&c=&ch=)

#6 - Imagine electrons jiggling all around you, seemingly and perhaps actually, "moving in and out of existence" ([quantum physics/wave-particle duality](https://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=001Js7i-nacKIxVEi2qGbJVQ9QNY856e4sUjJutnYwYpoI7g08RctneKbwsiBzgiw9Wh5EZ4sqSMolzQaBuxY7DQMinGviZ1xZ_5wGcqWmgkY-6EDzFyKN02dUPWGDwQ6CBB8FZQr6wTWYbV1t5afaTVuuuj60phKjg3b-UQ8ZKBpIk1J4ZAd9XYtmmGuODLknnoAuu48aDrpHwh8vboyhYrw==&c=&ch=))

#7 - Look for simplicities, patterns, and relationships in two disparate items. The more weird and seemingly unrelated they are, the more rewarding this exercise often is (ex. [what would happen to air pollution in a black hole](https://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=001Js7i-nacKIxVEi2qGbJVQ9QNY856e4sUjJutnYwYpoI7g08RctneKbwsiBzgiw9W82KlQGkDCHMgNrnrz3PvW016Lcof721nN21qiwNpFVx7bjtoPjVgztwZCpRnc6760FbCgF3TA9RaHAHWpAglPA==&c=&ch=)?)

#8 - Look at anything and instead of seeing matter see data.

#9 - Count the number of fundamental forces acting on you. There's only 4! It's that simple! Gravity . . . Electromagnetic . . . Strong nuclear . . . Weak nuclear. Most of what people think as separate forces are actually all part of the same force--the electromagnetic force. [The forces underlying nature are much much simpler than most people think,](https://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=001Js7i-nacKIxVEi2qGbJVQ9QNY856e4sUjJutnYwYpoI7g08RctneKXYuBhknKsE6gmgf9RsnO0blTBdrggnKXYs10UazqBUrMgdI-B5yowAmnA43QnwswXyC4lzmo7AthjKdvmd22V84YXxdULqDCLD-SALiRKSR&c=&ch=) and its critical we remind ourselves constantly how simple it is.

#10 - [And this is #1 for me and the calisthenic I struggle with the most to exercise] God. Oneness with God. This I think is the biggest sense of reality we are capable of sensing--though I do not have proof and must pursue it through faith. The continual presence of Christ. "It is no longer I who live, but Christ who lives in me." I can't image there being a bigger sense of reality than to be one with God.
