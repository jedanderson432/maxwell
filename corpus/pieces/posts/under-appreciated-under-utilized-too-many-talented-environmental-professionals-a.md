Too many talented environmental professionals are being put on the sidelines, or fully retiring when they don't fully want to . . . or feeling trapped in job circumstances. It's Covid. It's the economy. It's a changing job world.

First, a message of truth and hope. Your true value has nothing to do with work. Your value is you. It's the eternal you. Humans take time far more seriously than eternity. We are silly in this way. Conforming or accepting the world's judgment of our worth is a sin. It's as bad as any other sin. Don't do it. As Kierkegaard said, the world's judgment should mean nothing to us. . . "except as unconscious sarcasm." Love the world and the people in it . . . but we are not allowed to accept its judgment.

Second, a job offer. We have massive amounts of work to do to build this AI environmental protection system. We will give you a shot. Everyone has talents we need. [Contact us today](mailto:jed@enviro.ai) if you want to help build the environmental future of tomorrow.
