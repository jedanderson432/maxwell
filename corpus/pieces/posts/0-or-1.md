Everything in the universe is essentially 'deciding' between a zero or a one in a field of probabilities. At a more fundamental level we’re learning nature really is [acting in this strange but simple](https://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=0010wiZs0VbAW29E-NRBBSM_Hto1w5fREkNjKAFEMsjoOQI5PRESBpNFBKTpXCOrtjdRaOr8wwANG18w8qqnVGrc5Az5-tIjdOudYdvipFfFXlWh3ZcFB1HHT8Xg8LfPfH3zJg0BCH9SLoaMxK37Zvhe3qVj2quAiauG3e9WXzVPTeG8erhmqn2MQ==&c=&ch=) . . . almost [c](https://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=0010wiZs0VbAW29E-NRBBSM_Hto1w5fREkNjKAFEMsjoOQI5PRESBpNFBKTpXCOrtjdEHz7ZTvD7uLzoE9ircmpi1X1a7fEl9jEn8d5qAFeZCsAK9kMoPhh7iHifMK4G01IGxfSdF81ng9fV-ryDSbcNo0PohnrufOBJDekyEIpkP8=&c=&ch=)[o](https://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=0010wiZs0VbAW29E-NRBBSM_Hto1w5fREkNjKAFEMsjoOQI5PRESBpNFBKTpXCOrtjdEHz7ZTvD7uLzoE9ircmpi1X1a7fEl9jEn8d5qAFeZCsAK9kMoPhh7iHifMK4G01IGxfSdF81ng9fV-ryDSbcNo0PohnrufOBJDekyEIpkP8=&c=&ch=)[mputational manner](https://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=0010wiZs0VbAW29E-NRBBSM_Hto1w5fREkNjKAFEMsjoOQI5PRESBpNFBKTpXCOrtjdEHz7ZTvD7uLzoE9ircmpi1X1a7fEl9jEn8d5qAFeZCsAK9kMoPhh7iHifMK4G01IGxfSdF81ng9fV-ryDSbcNo0PohnrufOBJDekyEIpkP8=&c=&ch=).
