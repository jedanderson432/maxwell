Quantum Literacy in the Environmental Profession

Soon everyone in the environmental profession will have access to quantum computing (see some of the latest headlines and articles below). The question is . . . will everyone in the environmental profession be prepared with questions to ask a quantum computer about how to optimize different facets of environmental protection? For that, I'm urging everyone in the environmental profession gain some basic quantum literacy. It will help you immensely to formulate questions and prepare for opportunities.
