. . . and I thought learning quantum physics and machine-learning was hard :)

EnviroAI's capital raise has now jumped from $10 Million to $100 Million.

I find no issue more boring and frustrating than money . . . but it must be dealt with. I spend most of my days now talking about terms like EBITDA and cap tables. I can't wait to get past this stage.

Good news though on this difficult subject. Our annual revenue projection based on the latest independent financial modeling now is $868,319,601 in 2027. That I believe should put us on a good economic glide-path toward successfully completing our mission.

More prayers please as we walk through this--especially on the difficult issue of money.

---"We've got to make other people lots of money . . .

- Jed Anderson, EnviroAI

The fastest way for EnviroAI to complete our mission to build a 'machine' to optimize and ensure the protection of earth's ecosystems is to make other people lots and lots of money.

---"When people make lots of money, they want to make even more money . . . which in turn will allow us to speed even faster toward accomplishing our mission."

- Jed Anderson, CEO, EnviroAI
