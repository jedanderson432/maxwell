---"With the development of 'Pathways', EnviroAI will be able to more easily train a multi-tasked general intelligence AI system to perform various environmental activities spanning different disciplines and capabilities:

- Modelling
- Auditing
- Inventorying
- Monitoring
- Compliance Tasks
- Due Diligence
- Permit Preparation
- Reporting
- Investigations
- Inspections


---It will be much easier for us at EnviroAI to use general AI rather than narrow AI to train models and complete our automation of these environmental protection tasks as we continue building a system to help protect the environment, nature, and humanity."
