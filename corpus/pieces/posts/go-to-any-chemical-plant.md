Go to your customer's facility . . . (in TX, LA, and soon other states)

. . . without even walking through the fence .

No gates to walk through.

No security videos to watch.

Less safety and operational concerns for the company than a physical visit.

![](/images/posts/go-to-any-chemical-plant/img-04.gif)

Thanks to exponentially advancing technology . . . much more work can be done remotely and virtually!

Safer.

Less expensive. (ex./ paying you to drive there and then accompanying you through the facility if you are a paid consultant or contractor doing more off-site related work)

Often Much Faster . . . way to get certain information and get work done.
