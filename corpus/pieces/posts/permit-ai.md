How would Permit AI work?

- Permit AI would front-load the permitting process as much as possible. The initial permit submission package would include the application, an initial application review (APIRT), modelling (we hope to be close to being able to perform real-time modelling with our partners in the near future), BACT review, and the draft permit.
- Permit AI would include verification hyperlinks to agency support and other supporting documents for regulators to verify the artificial intelligence algorithms and outputs.
- Artificial intelligence technologies such as NLP, NLG, search, vision, and advanced analytics would be used to help create the to
- Permit AI would take advantage of Ceres AI’s current databases and capabilities.
- The primary goal would be to get as close as possible to “push a button . . . get a permit” excluding the public notice requirements.
