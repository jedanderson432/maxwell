Goal:  Zero-Emissions Industry on Earth

Industry doesn't want to pollute. They've never wanted to pollute. What's fascinating is that we are arriving at an inflection point in environmental history. It's becoming cheaper with technological innovation to proactively address one's own pollution on one's own terms than re-actively respond to laws and rules. It's very different than environmental progress from the 1960's-2010's (statutory and rules based).

Beyond compliance. Beyond regulation. Beyond wonderful.
