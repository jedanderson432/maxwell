. . . Yes, I know that none of us are physicists, but why is it important for professional and personal reasons to take a few minutes to grasp quantum entanglement?

It changes you. And humanity is just starting to harness its powers.



---"After you learn quantum mechanics . . . you're never really the same again."

-Steven Weinberg, University of Texas

﻿


The statement above of Professor Weinberg's at the University of Texas I've found to be true. It's impossible to grasp the fundamentals of quantum mechanics and look at the world in the same way. The universe becomes that much more of a magical place filled with endless possibilities . . . or I should say . . . probabilities.



----"Quantum entanglement is likely the most disturbing yet enlightening physical phenomena you will ever have a chance to grasp before you leave this earth. And thinking through how we can prepare ourselves to utilize its powers to better understand and optimize our protection of nature is exhilarating. It's an incredible world we are living into."

- Jed Anderson, Creator, EnviroAI





Humanity is beginning to harness the weird world of quantum entanglement. It won't be just physicists using it. It will be you and me and artificial intelligence--which also is based on probability.
