Technology in the 1st industrial revolution started pollution.

Technology in the next industrial revolution will end pollution . . . and save nature.

Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computational Methods/Communication Technology, and Advanced Remote Sensing

Nature is data. Nature is math. Nature is an algorithm. As human data, math, and algorithms have advanced exponentially in recent years, our understanding of nature too has advanced. Although we've made great progress, the limitations inherent in traditional/classical systems understanding and protecting a quantum system have become more pronounced. Now however we are beginning to move toward a quantum system to understand and protect a quantum system. Data measurement technologies are advancing (i.e. satellites, fence-line systems, mobile sensors). Greater connectivity is occurring between machines and between humans-and-machines (IoT, 5G). Finally, vast data sets can now be managed and mined with the help of artificial intelligence that previously were too large and unwieldy for humans to draw useful information from.

Humanity is beginning to enter an environmental renaissance period.

Simplified algorithms will replace the need for reams of textual regulation that currently must be translated from human language to machine language back to human language back to machine language. Environmental protection is about to get much, much simpler.

It's an incredible world we are living into. The hope of the natural world and humanity has never been brighter.
