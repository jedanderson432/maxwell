I've been asked for details from some of the thousands of you about the design of Permit.AI

Permit.AI

‌

- Permit.AI would perform not only most of the applicant’s process, but many of the current agency permitting tasks.

- Agency hyperlinks and review algorithms would be provided for agency review and verification purposes.

- A citizen app with AI sentient analysis would be developed for early comment, review, and incorporation purposes.

- Agencies could choose to ignore the AI, but value would be derived regardless--especially for those companies with Environmental Social Governance investment concerns.
