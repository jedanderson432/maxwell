Stimulating human environmental problem solving---Enviro AI

--"For 20 years . . .

I had to visualize in my head where emission points were located, what they looked like, and what other sources/receptors were located around them . . . and then make the mental jump and link this with emissions, control, compliance, or regulatory information. . . .

Now I can see all of it together in seconds."- Jed Anderson, Creator, Enviro AI

VISUALIZE

----"By visualizing information, we turn it into a landscape that you can explore with your eyes, a sort of information map.” ―David McCandless

---“Visualizing data changes how data are understood and increases interest in data generally, which will encourage more and better data to be developed.” – Robert Kosara

---“The art and practice of visualizing data is becoming ever more important in bridging the human-computer gap to mediate analytical insight in a meaningful way.” ―Edd Dumbill

---“Visualization gives you answers to questions you didn’t know you had.”—Ben Schneiderman
