Status Report

Building a Flood Intelligence System:

Status Report

![](/images/posts/flood-ai/img-01.png)

No one is interested in a building a flood intelligence system. We can't build it if no one wants it.

I can pray for my community when it comes to flooding, but I can't help protect my community if they don't want my help.

EnviroAI has offered to build a flood artificial intelligence system that is jurisdictionally-agnostic and data-driven. I've even offered to personally pay for it. I'll continue to offer my service to my community, and in the mean time, I'll pray.

----“Service to humanity is service to God.” – Lailah Gifty Akita
