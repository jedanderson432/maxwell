Why am I in such a hurry to build a machine to protect nature and humanity?








---"There are two days in my calendar: This day and that Day."

- Martin Luther


In thinking about reality, and what is possible under the laws of physics, one constraint I personally face is time. I will soon die. It's a physical fact. Perhaps today. I don't find this morose. To the contrary, I find it liberating, joyous, centering, and motivational. I try to keep it constantly before me.




How much love and energy can I put into the universe before I leave it?
