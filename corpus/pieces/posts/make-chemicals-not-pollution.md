Outsourcing environmental treatment

‌

This goes out to 10,000 of you.


Get out of the pollution business

Send your emissions, wastewater, and solid/hazardous waste offsite.





Make chemicals . . . not pollution

The initial reaction to this project from some of my clients has been . . . "I can't imagine life without my flare and wastewater plant. I love my flare. I love my wastewater treatment plant.




I keep reminding my clients that these units do not make them money. It must always come down to cost. Is it cheaper to keep treating my own pollution, or is it better now to outsource this task?
