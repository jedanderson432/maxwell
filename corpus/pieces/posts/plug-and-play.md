Augmented Environmental Intelligence

Once completed, selecting this button will pull up your facility(s) in EnviroAI . . . and engage your internal Environmental Management Information System (EMIS) if you wish (either ours, yours, or a 3rd party vendors). Firewalls will remain intact with only one-way transmittal of data (i.e. external data informing internal data). The feature will essentially be plug-and-play.
