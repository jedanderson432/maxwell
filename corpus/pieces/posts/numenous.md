Metamorphosis

Humans & Nature

--"We are perhaps at the most significant inflection point in the history of humans and nature.

Never before have humans been here. And never after will we be here again. For the [first 1.7 million years](https://r20.rs6.net/tn.jsp?f=001xDbNOgxzejNExlDv7ahs1e94b7Q8n_jgiSMNibGEzykQMKLb0ZddfyAy1imwWVC3vANcSMNnlgr1Z4TKm2gcnpkIXGUE5NmurhQQG5t_BDcBRX8h53jKzW3iF_Umc0NGhSo1Mum0OdmZpxIjtNClgcyYMcqajK-eL2iUfJu-3luXAuKBOPQ0-5c3IcAddFByNbX8_ZYwEGli6J1bfF_pdYJjmgi9uGC2mhnhgdchZRh6nlndVCJ1L9siEE8skp9u9Fm44woBurSLPXcmPno3Bg0Wxf_xBhxEq4yB1dz9hoYo2iFHgKDNZ5RaZJJkgu3yI-swuC0j8RzPPhsb3-wQWZm_wN8Mo7UP8MrTTWWsGwI=&c=&ch=), human technology hurt nature. Now technology is metamorphosing into a role that might save nature in the first place.

Humans up to this point in time have viewed themselves as bad for nature. Now we realize we were intended for good for nature--we were just "bad learners". It takes a long time to learn--and when you're learning you make a lot of mistakes. Perhaps we will one day learn that God gave us brains that we might evolve to use them to save nature for God. Redemptive. Transcendent. Numinous."----Jed Anderson, Creator, EnviroAI
