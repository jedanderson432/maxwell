1. MORE DATA.

EnviroAI presents more state data than states themselves present state environmental data (we obtain additional data through public information act requests] [ex. we present 20% more data than the State of Texas]

2. SMARTER DATA.

EnviroAI uses advanced artificial intelligence to make state data even more useful, insightful, and accessible to customers.

3. FASTER DATA.

EnviroAI employs advanced information search and retrieval systems focusing specifically on information transfer speed (i.e. "time is money")
