Why can't I build this machine to protect humanity and nature faster?

Money

I personally hate money. I find it highly annoying. It buys a bunch of problems I don't want. Part of the challenge of building this company is to make it a success without me becoming a financial success.

$20k a month

I can only spend max $20k cash a month on development at this point. That's all I've got. And in fact more than I've got. Here's a fun new fact though . . . you can do amazing things right now without much money. The only true limit is becoming the limits of imagination. Money is decreasingly becoming a barrier. It's an incredible new world we are living into!
