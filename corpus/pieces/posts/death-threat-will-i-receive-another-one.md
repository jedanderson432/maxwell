I won't be intimidated.

As a follow-up to yesterday's message on love and beauty . . . some people think that the death threat and hate mail they've sent will silence me about talking about the inefficiencies of the environmental regulatory system and working to replace it.

It won't. I won't. Love. We can do better.

We are at the beginning of an environmental renaissance period in an age of artificial intelligence and remote sensing. Soon others will begin to see these new technologies and the new hope and new possibility in the relationship between humans and nature.

HAVE COURAGE. ALL IS WELL.
