Faith. Faith. Faith------Building a system to help protect earth and humanity---EnviroAI


Another $60,000 from my retirement account . . .

EnviroAI added two more Fortune 500 companies as customers in the past month, but I just had to go into my retirement another $60,000 for my family to live on as we build this system to save earth and humanity--and to help companies and the environment.

One of my former pastors last Sunday asked me "how do you do this?" I told him faith.


This email is not a "woe is me". Help will arrive. I've asked God and others to help. I'm confident that each of us will receive if we believe that what we've asked for we will receive. I hope each of you does the same in your prayer life and trying to do your best to ascertain and do the will of God. We ain't perfect. Neither are our intentions. But God is. We can trust in that.

Keep asking and seeking and knocking and doing All is well. All will be well.
